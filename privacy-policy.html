This Privacy Policy describes how SteelCrest Surplus Co. LLC ("SteelCrest," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our business operations and internal automated SMS notification system.

SMS Alerts — Opt-In & Consent

SteelCrest operates an internal automated SMS alert system that sends business notifications exclusively to the business owner/operator. This is not a marketing program. No messages are sent to customers, third parties, or the general public.

How Opt-In Consent Is Obtained

The sole recipient of SMS messages is Joshua Hairston, owner of SteelCrest Surplus Co. LLC. Consent is obtained when the owner configures the SteelCrest internal dashboard by entering their mobile phone number and checking the following agreement:

Message sender: +1 (978) 958-9865
Message frequency: Up to 10 messages per day depending on scan results and business activity.
Message & data rates may apply.
To opt out: Reply STOP to any message. You will receive one confirmation and no further messages.
For help: Reply HELP or contact steelcrestsurplus@gmail.com
SMS opt-in data and mobile numbers are never shared with or sold to third parties for marketing purposes.
SMS opt-in information, including mobile phone numbers collected for this program, is never shared with or sold to any third party for marketing or any other purpose.

Types of SMS Messages Sent

The following message types may be sent to the registered business owner number:

  • Deal alerts — when the automated scanner identifies a profitable arbitrage opportunity.
  • Purchase confirmations — when an item is purchased or sourced.
  • Listing updates — when an item is published, updated, or sold on a marketplace.
  • Daily scan summaries — a once-daily roundup of scan results and business activity.

Information We Collect

Because the SMS system serves a single internal recipient, the personal information we collect is limited. We may collect and store:

  • The business owner's mobile phone number, provided for the purpose of receiving SMS alerts.
  • SMS delivery metadata (timestamps, delivery status, and opt-in/opt-out status) generated by our messaging provider.
  • Business operational data (inventory, pricing, listing, and scan data) generated by our own systems, which is not personal information about any individual.

We do not collect mobile numbers or personal information from customers, the public, or any third parties through this SMS system.

How We Use Information

Information collected is used solely to:

  • Deliver the internal SMS alerts described above to the business owner.
  • Process opt-out (STOP) and help (HELP) requests.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve SteelCrest's internal business automation systems.
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not use this information for marketing to any party, and we do not perform automated profiling of any individual beyond delivering the operational alerts described.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information — including mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in data — with third parties for marketing or any other purpose.

The only third party with access to message data is our SMS delivery provider:

  • Twilio Inc. — used solely as the telecommunications carrier/provider to transmit SMS messages to the registered business owner number. Twilio processes message content and the recipient number only to deliver messages on our behalf, subject to Twilio's own privacy and security obligations.

No mobile information is shared with any other third party. SMS opt-in consent and phone numbers are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

Data Retention

We retain the business owner's mobile number and opt-in status for as long as the internal SMS alert system is in active use. SMS delivery metadata is retained only as long as necessary for operational, troubleshooting, and legal-compliance purposes, after which it is deleted. Upon opt-out, the mobile number is flagged as opted-out and no further messages are sent; the number may be removed from the system entirely upon request.

Opt-Out & Recipient Rights

The message recipient may opt out of SMS alerts at any time by replying STOP to any message. Upon receiving a STOP request, we will send a single confirmation message and send no further messages to that number. To resume messages, the recipient may reply START or re-enroll through the dashboard. For assistance, reply HELP or contact us at the email below.

The recipient may also request access to, correction of, or deletion of their stored personal information by contacting us at steelcrestsurplus@gmail.com.
